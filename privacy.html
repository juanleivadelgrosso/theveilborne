PRIVACY POLICY

Veilborne Uploader · Last updated: 7 November 2026

"Veilborne Uploader" (the "App") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel The Veilborne to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public and no other person can sign in to it.

What data the App accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the App requests these YouTube permissions for the owner's own channel only:

  • Upload and manage videos (youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl): to upload the channel's own videos, set their title, description, tags and subtitles, and schedule them.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to list the channel's own videos and confirm uploads.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): to read the channel's own view, watch time and retention statistics.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and schedule the channel's videos and to measure their performance so that future videos can be improved. The App does not access any other user's data, does not show ads and does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.

Where the data is stored

The sign-in token and the statistics are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's YouTube APIs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer removes all stored access.
